DUCK BUDGET — SHOPPING FIRST + MEAL PLAN UPDATE (OCTOBER 2026)

WHAT CHANGED
- Shopping now starts on Shopping List; the Meal Plan is a separate top-level section within Shopping.
- The shopping list supports + New Item, price, reusable saved items, editing, checkboxes, and automatic grouping of matching names (case/spacing ignored).
- Meal Plan has Weekly Meal Plan and Saved Meals subtabs. All seven Sun–Sat days have Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks slots.
- Saved recipes are assigned a meal type and can be added directly to any day. Each scheduled copy can be edited, removed, or moved independently of its saved template.
- Recipes do not automatically add to the shopping list or increase the weekly shopping budget. This supports shopping before deciding what to cook.
- The + Missing ingredients action on Weekly Meal Plan compares ingredient name quantities with shopping-list entries and only adds additional entries if needed. Repeated use avoids duplicating already-listed items. Ingredients with the same name combine in the list.
- Weekly budget, weekly list total, and remaining amount are visible at the top. Previous/next week buttons work independently of saved items.
- Older shopping lists retain their existing entries. On loading an older saved week for the first time, meal ingredients are copied ONCE into the standalone shopping list, keeping their costs and checkmarks; editing recipes afterwards will not change this list.
- For older recipes without a meal type, Dinner is the initial category. You can reclassify any old recipe from Saved Meals > Edit and any planned meal by editing its day.
- Existing bills, DailyPay, budgets, goals, backups, local storage, and Duckie Days art remain intact.

INSTALL
1. Export a budget-data backup from Settings in the CURRENT app first.
2. Extract the ZIP and upload its CONTENTS (not the ZIP or a nested folder) to the repository root.
3. Commit and wait for GitHub Pages to deploy; refresh the installed app if it shows a cached screen.

The budget and meal costs here are planning estimates; no actual transactions are automatically recorded.
